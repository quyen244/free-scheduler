Campaign state machine

Failure is a state, not a dead end.

needs_action and partial_failure preserve work and expose a safe next action. Only rejected, failed_terminal, and completed end the campaign.

submitted

URL and rights arrive.

validating

Identity, media, duration, resolution.

processing

Transcript, metadata, voice, renders.

ready_for_review

Complete manifest and revision.

approval_pending

One Telegram decision.

publishing

Independent platform targets.

completed

Published or draft-delivered as contracted.

Recovery branches: processing can enter retry_wait or needs_action; publishing can enter partial_failure. Resume from the failed boundary, never erase successful peers.
Database relationship

Stable identity, immutable revisions, append-only attempts.

Video bytes stay in local storage. Databases keep identities, paths, hashes, state transitions, approvals, attempts, and audit history.

Failure and support contract

Every error answers: what happened, what survived, what next.

The system records every attempt, but Telegram interrupts the operator only when automatic recovery is exhausted or a decision is necessary.

Failure classExamplesSystem behaviorUser support
TransientTimeout, 429, provider 5xxUp to three backoff retries.Show attempt count; alert only after exhaustion.
Invalid inputCorrupt, below 720p, outside 5–20 minStop before expensive processing.Explain the rule and allow replacement.
Missing configurationRights, music, expired credentialNo blind retry.Open the exact setting or reconnect action.
Invalid outputWrong ratio, missing audio, corrupt renderRetry affected render once, then pause.Retry failed stage; preserve transcript and metadata.
Stale revisionEdit after approvalInvalidate old approval.Prepare and approve the new revision.
Partial publishOne TikTok target fails after YouTube succeedsKeep successes and retry only failure.Show the exact target and safe retry action.
Unknown outcomeTimeout after provider received bytesReconcile by provider/session ID.Never create a duplicate upload blindly.

Retention: temporary files are removed one day after completion, source/final files after seven days, and failed-campaign files after three days. Metadata and audit history remain until manual deletion. Active references are never deleted.
